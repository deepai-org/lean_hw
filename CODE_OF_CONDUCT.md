# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in Loom should be safe, constructive, and technically honest.
Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- treat people with respect regardless of identity, background, experience,
  or viewpoint;
- critique claims, proofs, code, and evidence rather than people;
- welcome counterexamples and good-faith challenges to theorem statements;
- disclose uncertainty, trust assumptions, and conflicts of interest;
- avoid harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption,
  doxxing, and publication of private information.

These expectations apply in repository discussions, reviews, project chat,
and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `kevin@deepai.org`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent practical. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, impose a temporary ban, or
permanently exclude a participant according to the severity and pattern of
conduct. Anyone directly involved in a report must recuse themselves from its
resolution where another reviewer is available.

Good-faith reports and appeals will not be retaliated against. An enforcement
decision may be appealed by replying with relevant context or evidence.
